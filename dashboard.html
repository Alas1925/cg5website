Strengthening Maritime Partnerships

The Office of the Deputy Chief of Coast Guard Staff for International Affairs (CG-5) supports the Philippine Coast Guard in strengthening international cooperation, maritime partnerships, and strategic engagement with foreign coast guard organizations and international maritime partners.

International Affairs

The International Affairs function of the Philippine Coast Guard facilitates coordination and engagement with international partners in support of maritime cooperation and common objectives.

OUR DIRECTION

Mission & Vision

The Office of International Affairs continues to strengthen the Philippine Coast Guard's engagement with international maritime partners through meaningful cooperation, professional relationships, and sustained international presence.

M
MISSION

Our Mission

To support the Philippine Coast Guard in developing, coordinating, and sustaining international maritime engagements that promote cooperation, mutual understanding, professional development, and stronger relationships with foreign coast guard organizations and international partners.

V
VISION

Our Vision

A globally connected and professionally recognized Philippine Coast Guard that maintains strong maritime partnerships and contributes to a safe, secure, peaceful, and cooperative maritime environment.
